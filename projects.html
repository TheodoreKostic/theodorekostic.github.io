Projects

This page presents selected theoretical and computational projects related to solar physics and polarized radiative transfer. It will document the underlying physical models, numerical methods, and validation of the results.

Polarized Radiative Transfer and the Hanle Effect

This project explores the theoretical description and numerical modeling of polarized radiation in the solar atmosphere, with particular emphasis on the Hanle effect and the influence of magnetic fields on scattering polarization.

Objectives

  • Understand the theoretical framework for polarized radiation and atomic polarization.
  • Implement the relevant equations and tensor transformations in Python.
  • Validate numerical results against analytical expressions and published reference calculations.
  • Develop a foundation for more comprehensive models of polarized radiative transfer.

Methods

The computational work uses Python and numerical methods to investigate radiation anisotropy, magnetic-field geometry, polarization tensors, and the resulting Stokes parameters. JAX implementation is coming soon.

Current status

Ongoing work: implementing and validating the theoretical formulation, with the aim of extending it toward polarized radiative transfer calculations. Trying to translate this to the case of polarized radiative transfer in the solar atmosphere (in contrast to the case of observer above the solar limb); we are using the same coordinate systems as in the previous case. This time we will precompute \(J^K_Q\) tensors for the radiation field.
