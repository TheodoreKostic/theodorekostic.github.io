About

I am a Research Assistant at the Astronomical Observatory in Belgrade, Serbia.

My research interests include solar physics, particularly polarized radiative transfer and the theoretical interpretation of polarization in spectral lines :).

I am interested in understanding how radiation interacts with matter in solar atmosphere, and how these interactions can be used to investigate the physical conditions in which the spectral line forms. Naturally, given the nature of solar photosphere, I am interested in how the magnetic field affects plasma, Zeeman and Hanle effects and spectral lines that are susceptible to these.
